# FixTrack Support

FixTrack helps repair shops and retail stores check in customers, track repair and sale tickets, and keep warranty records.

## Contact

Email: ahmedmilon1992@gmail.com

Please include your store name and a short description of the problem. A screenshot helps.

## Common questions

**I forgot the staff PIN.** Email us from the email address used for the store login and we will help you reset it.

**I cannot sign in.** Check that the device has an internet connection and that the email and password are the ones used when the store was set up.

**How do I add a technician or change the store logo?** Open the menu at the top right of the ticket list, choose Store settings, and enter the staff PIN.

**A customer wants their details corrected or removed.** Email us with the store name and the customer's phone number or email address.

**I want to close my store account.** Email us from the account's email address and we will delete the account and its data.
