# FixTrack Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

FixTrack is an app that repair shops and retail stores use to check in customers, keep repair and sale tickets, and keep warranty records. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

## Who this policy covers

- **Stores:** businesses that create a FixTrack account.
- **Customers:** people who enter their details on a store's device when they visit that store.

## Information we collect

**From stores:** account email and password (the password is stored in protected form and we cannot read it), store name, address, phone number, email, an optional logo, and technician names.

**From customers, entered at the store:** name, phone number and email address, plus the record that the customer agreed to the store's check-in terms, including the date, time and the wording they agreed to.

**Ticket records created by the store:** type of visit (repair or sale), device details, notes, price, warranty period, dates and times, the technician's name, and an optional photo of the receipt.

FixTrack does not collect location, contacts, browsing history or advertising identifiers, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

## How the information is used

- To let the store keep service and warranty records and find them later.
- To let the store contact its own customers, including offers by text or email when the customer agreed to that.
- To keep accounts secure and the service working.

## Who can see it

- Each store can see only its own customers and tickets. A company that owns several stores can see the records of its own stores.
- We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with advertisers.
- Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, and is sent over encrypted connections.

## The store's role

The store you visit decides what it collects and how it contacts you. For questions about a specific visit, warranty or message, contact that store first.

## Offers and messages

If you agreed to receive offers, you can stop them at any time. Reply STOP to a text message, use the unsubscribe link in an email, or ask the store.

## Keeping and deleting information

Records are kept while the store's account is active, because they serve as warranty records.

- **Customers** can ask the store, or email us, to correct or delete their details.
- **Stores** can ask us to delete their account and its data by emailing us from the account's email address.

## Children

FixTrack is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
